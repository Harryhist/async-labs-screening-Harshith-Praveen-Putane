# Video/Reel Storyboard: "Calendar Clarity"
## 15–30 Second Runtime

---

## OVERALL ARC
**Problem** → **Tension** → **Product Reveal** → **Relief** → **CTA**

A busy professional wakes up stressed about meetings. They check their desk and see the smart display already showing their calendar. Immediate calm. CTA.

---

## SHOT-BY-SHOT BREAKDOWN

### SHOT 1: The Problem (0–3 seconds)
**Visual:**  
- Open on a close-up of a smartphone screen showing Google Calendar
- Multiple overlapping meeting notifications ping in rapid succession
- Red warning notifications, audible "ding ding ding" sounds
- Phone buzzes in hand

**Text Overlay:**  
- "Your calendar is a mess."

**Audio:**  
- Stressed notification sounds (or soft, discordant music)

**Tone:** Anxious, chaotic

---

### SHOT 2: The Confusion (3–6 seconds)
**Visual:**  
- Cut to a person (seated at desk, early morning) looking confused/stressed
- They're checking their phone, then their laptop, trying to figure out their day
- Frantic scrolling
- Close-up of their face: eyebrows furrowed, slight frown

**Text Overlay:**  
- "You check your phone."
- "You check your laptop."
- "You still don't know if you're free."

**Audio:**  
- Keyboard clicks, phone taps (quick, staccato)
- Subtle tension music building

**Tone:** Frustrated, overwhelmed

---

### SHOT 3: The Discovery (6–9 seconds)
**Visual:**  
- Camera pans to reveal the 7.5" smart display on the desk
- The display shows a clean, clear calendar grid for the day
- All meetings visible at a glance
- Soft, warm lighting on the display

**Text Overlay:**  
- "Until you see this."

**Audio:**  
- Music shifts: tension releases slightly
- Soft "chime" or positive notification sound
- Calming background music begins

**Tone:** Hopeful, lighter

---

### SHOT 4: The Realization (9–12 seconds)
**Visual:**  
- Close-up of the person's face: eyes widen, expression softens
- They lean back in their chair, visibly relaxed
- A subtle smile appears
- Cut back to the display showing the calendar clearly

**Text Overlay:**  
- "Your entire day. Visible. Always on."

**Audio:**  
- Calm, warm background music
- Optional: subtle "whoosh" or transition sound

**Tone:** Relieved, confident

---

### SHOT 5: The Integration Demo (12–18 seconds, OPTIONAL for longer video)
**Visual:**  
- Split screen or quick cut: person adds a meeting on their phone
- Instantly, it appears on the desk display
- Multiple examples: meeting added → display updates (3 rapid examples)

**Text Overlay:**  
- "Real-time sync with Google Calendar."
- "Slack. Notion. All your tools."

**Audio:**  
- Quick, light notification sounds (positive/satisfied)
- Upbeat music continues

**Tone:** Efficient, reassuring

---

### SHOT 6: The Lifestyle Moment (18–24 seconds)
**Visual:**  
- Wide shot: the person at their desk, working peacefully
- Display visible in corner, showing calendar
- They're focused, typing, calm
- Soft natural/warm office lighting
- Optional: a meeting notification appears on the display (not their phone)
- They glance at the display, nod, continue working

**Text Overlay:**  
- "Never miss another meeting."
- "Never stress about your calendar again."

**Audio:**  
- Gentle, confident background music
- Optional: very soft keyboard sounds (ambient, not frantic)

**Tone:** Aspirational, peaceful

---

### SHOT 7: The CTA (24–30 seconds)
**Visual:**  
- Product shot: clean angle of the smart display on a minimalist desk
- Display showing calendar, maybe with some weather/time info too
- Fade to simple, clean screen with:
  - Product image
  - Headline text
  - CTA button/link

**Text Overlay:**  
- "Stop Missing Meetings"
- "Shop Now" or "Learn More"
- Small: "7.5" Smart Display | Real-time Sync | 30-day Battery"

**Audio:**  
- Music reaches satisfying resolution (slight crescendo then calm)
- Soft fade out

**Tone:** Confident, clear, direct

---

## TECHNICAL NOTES

### Camera & Framing
- **Shots 1–2:** Tight on phone/laptop screens, close-ups of person
- **Shots 3–4:** Medium wide to medium close (show desk + person + display)
- **Shots 5–6:** Medium to wide (lifestyle, context)
- **Shot 7:** Product hero shot, clean background

### Lighting
- **Shots 1–2:** Slightly desaturated, cooler tones (feels stressful)
- **Shots 3–7:** Warm, natural light; well-lit product (feels calm, premium)
- **Transition:** Subtle color grade shift at Shot 3 (cooler → warmer)

### Music/Sound Design
- **Intro (0–3s):** Tense, discordant, notification sounds
- **Mid (3–9s):** Tension building, staccato
- **Peak (9–12s):** Release, shift to calm
- **Resolution (12–30s):** Warm, confident, ambient music

### Text Timing
- Each text overlay appears for 2–3 seconds
- Large font, white text with subtle dark background/shadow for readability
- Text appears on-screen smoothly (fade in), stays for duration, fades out

### Pacing
- **Shots 1–2:** Quick cuts, rapid movement (conveys chaos)
- **Shots 3–4:** Slower pacing (product reveal, moment of realization)
- **Shots 5–7:** Steady, confident pacing (resolution)
