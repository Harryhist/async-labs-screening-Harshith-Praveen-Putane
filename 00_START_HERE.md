### Phase 2: Video/Storyboard (1.5–2 hours)

#### 2.1 Option A: Film the Video (1.5–2 hours, if you can)
**Follow:** `VIDEO_STORYBOARD.md` (shot-by-shot guide)

**What you need:**
- Smartphone with calendar app (Google Calendar)
- Smart display or product photo
- Desktop/laptop setup
- Camera (phone is fine)
- Ring light or good natural light
- Video editing software: DaVinci Resolve (free) or CapCut

**Process:**
1. Record 7 shots as per storyboard (total 30–60 sec raw)
2. Edit down to 15–30 seconds
3. Add music (royalty-free from YouTube Audio Library)
4. Add text overlays with timings from storyboard
5. Export as MP4, 1080p, vertical (9:16) or horizontal (16:9)
6. Upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Vimeo (public)
7. Paste link in `work/video_link.txt`

**Alternatively:** Create the storyboard as-is in the markdown (totally valid!)
