# T7 Smart Display - Ad Campaign Strategic Brief

---

## STEP 1: THREE AD CONCEPTS

### Concept A: "Calendar Clarity"
**Angle:** Always-visible calendar + priority management for busy professionals  
**Who it's for:** Product managers, founders, executives who context-switch constantly  
**Core message:** "Stop missing meetings. Your calendar is now impossible to ignore."  
**Tone:** Urgent, empowering  
**Visual vibe:** Desk setup, early morning, person glancing at the display with relief

---

### Concept B: "Calm Corner"
**Angle:** Ambient, beautiful desk companion for mental clarity + personalization  
**Who it's for:** Remote workers, creatives, wellness-conscious professionals  
**Core message:** "Your desk, finally designed for you. Calm, purposeful, always yours."  
**Tone:** Aspirational, mindful, aesthetic  
**Visual vibe:** Minimalist workspace, soft lighting, display showing art/quotes/water droplets

---

### Concept C: "Workflow Glue"
**Angle:** Real-time Slack/task sync; removes friction from checking phone or laptop  
**Who it's for:** Software engineers, designers, fast-paced distributed teams  
**Core message:** "Your team's work, one glance away. No more context switching."  
**Tone:** Technical, efficient, community-focused  
**Visual vibe:** Busy workspace, rapid task updates flowing, person staying in flow state

---

## STEP 2: WINNING CONCEPT

**Winner: Concept A — "Calendar Clarity"**

**Rationale:**  
Calendar management is a universal pain point (every professional struggles with it). It's specific enough to be memorable and visual in ads (you *see* a calendar vs. abstract "peace of mind"). The audience (execs/PMs) has disposable income and high meeting friction—they'll understand the value immediately. The problem is visceral (missed meetings cause real damage), which makes the solution feel urgent and worth buying today.

---

## STEP 3: TARGET AUDIENCE & A/B TEST

### Target Audience Profile
- **Who:** Product managers, startup founders, CTOs, busy C-suite (25–50 years old)
- **Context:** Spend 20+ hours/week in meetings; rely on calendar + Slack; remote or hybrid
- **Pain:** Meeting overlaps, timezone confusion, forgot to check calendar before booking back-to-backs
- **Desire:** Reduce cognitive load, stay calm under chaos, ship faster without admin stress
- **Income:** $80k–$250k+ (can afford $400–500 impulse/productivity buy)

### A/B Test Design
**Variable:** Headline messaging  
**A (Control):** "Stop Missing Meetings" (urgency/pain focus)  
**B (Test):** "Your Calendar, Impossible to Ignore" (positive/empowerment focus)  
**What stays the same:** Product image, sub-headline, CTA, visual design, audience targeting  
**Hypothesis:** Positive framing (B) will have higher click-through rate than pain-focused (A) because the audience is solutions-oriented, not fear-driven.
